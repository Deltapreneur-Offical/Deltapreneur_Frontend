Refund Policy

Last updated: 7th August 2026

This Refund Policy explains when and how Aultum International (operating as Deltapreneur) issues refunds for payments made in INR on https://hubregistrar.com for domain registrations, marketplace purchases, technology products, and related digital services.

1. Overview

Payments on hubregistrar.com are processed securely through authorized payment partners (including Razorpay and HDFC Bank SmartGateway and other RBI-regulated payment aggregators or banks enabled on our checkout).

2. When refunds are available

You may be eligible for a full or partial refund when:

  • Payment was captured but the service could not be fulfilled (for example, domain registration failed).
  • You cancelled in accordance with our Cancellation Policy before fulfillment completed.
  • A duplicate charge was made for the same order.
  • An unauthorized or erroneous charge attributable to a platform error.

3. Non-refundable items

  • Successfully registered domain names at the registry.
  • Digital goods or services already delivered or activated.
  • Completed marketplace transfers.
  • Fees marked non-refundable at checkout.

4. How to request a refund

Email support@hubregistrar.com or call +91 080 8575 8575 with your registered email/phone, order or payment ID, product name, and reason.

5. Refund process and timeline

We respond to refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are initiated within 2 business days of approval. Once initiated, refunds are typically credited to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days (up to 10 business days depending on your bank, card network, or UPI provider). Partial refunds may apply where only part of an order is reversed.

6. Original payment method

Refunds are returned to the same payment method used at checkout, in line with RBI and payment-network rules.

7. Failed or cancelled payments

If your account was debited but the order was not completed, verified refunds are processed within 5 business days (T+5) and should reflect within 5 to 7 business days thereafter.

8. Regulatory compliance

This policy is published under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. See also our Cancellation Policy, Terms and Conditions, and Privacy Policy.
