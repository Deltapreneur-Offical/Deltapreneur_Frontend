Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 7th August 2026

This Cancellation Policy explains how you can cancel an order or request before or after payment on HubRegistrar, operated by Aultum International, at https://hubregistrar.com. All prices are in INR.

1. Cancellation before payment

You may cancel anytime before completing checkout by removing cart items, closing the payment window, or abandoning an unpaid request. No charges apply before successful payment capture.

2. Cancellation after payment

  • If fulfillment has not completed, contact support immediately to stop fulfillment and receive a refund per our Refund Policy.
  • If fulfillment has completed (domain registered or digital product delivered), cancellation may not be possible; see the Refund Policy.

3. Cancellation timelines

For digital orders where fulfillment has not completed, submit a cancellation request within 24 hours of payment confirmation or before the service is marked delivered in your account—whichever is earlier. We confirm cancellation requests within 2 business days. Listing-specific terms on marketplace or auction pages may apply.

4. Subscriptions and renewals

Cancel future renewal periods by contacting support at least 7 calendar days before the next charge, subject to product terms at purchase.

5. Refunds after cancellation

Approved cancellations with a refund due are initiated within 2 business days. Amounts are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days (up to 10 business days), as described in our Refund Policy.

6. How to cancel

Email support@hubregistrar.com, call +91 080 8575 8575, or use the Contact Us page with your order/payment reference and registered contact details. We respond within 2 business days.

7. Regulatory compliance

Published under the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020. See also Refund Policy, Terms and Conditions, and Privacy Policy.
